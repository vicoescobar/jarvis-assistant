# ADHD UX & Prompt Engineering Rules

1. **Zero Friction (< 2 minutes):** Every notification or UI action must be completable in under 2 minutes. Limit morning briefings to 5 well-spaced sections (`Agenda`, `Mails importants`, `Tâches en cours`, `Trackers`, `Suggestion`).
2. **Direct Executive Tone (No Patronizing Language):** ADHD accommodation lives in the *architecture* (noise filtering, prioritization, low friction, proactive orchestration), NEVER in soft, coddling, or infantilizing copy. Strictly ban phrases like `"zéro pression"`, `"au calme"`, `"sans aucune culpabilité"`, `"si l'énergie n'y est pas"`, `"profite de cette liberté"`. Use a crisp, direct, factual chief-of-staff tone in French. Never downplay urgent items (bank/security alerts, deadlines, job leads).
3. **No Shame / No Streaks / No Night Amnesty / No "Plan Si/Alors":** Never implement red warning badges, broken streak counters, or "Plan Si/Alors" formulas in the morning briefing. Do NOT move, archive, or delete unfinished tasks from Google Tasks (`@default`) via automated night rollovers; surface all active tasks (including undated ones) clearly in the morning briefing.
4. **Ableton Live Sandbox Rule:** Frame music sessions strictly as 45-minute timed sandboxes (Sound Design OR Arrangement) ending with a mandatory 15-second MP3 export ritual (Peak-End Rule).
